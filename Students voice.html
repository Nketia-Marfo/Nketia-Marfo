STUDENT VOICES

Twenty journeys. Twenty perspectives. One conversation.

Student Voices brings together educational experiences, reflections, and lessons from people whose journeys have taken different paths across the world. Their stories explore education, opportunity, perseverance, career development, creativity, service, and the search for a meaningful future.

20

Different countries. Different journeys. Different lessons.

These voices remind us that there is no single path to growth or success. Some stories begin in classrooms, some begin with difficult circumstances, and others begin with a simple decision to keep learning.

01
🇬🇭

Renalta Aboagyewaa Nkansah

“My UHK/Erasmus Experience Changed My Life”

Renalta described how studying abroad through an exchange experience helped her become more confident, improve her communication and public-speaking abilities, and grow intellectually and socially. Her experience shows how an educational opportunity can change a student's outlook and personal development.

02
🇬🇭

Louis Agya Kwame Sottie

“Balancing Books and Leadership”

Louis shared his experience of combining strong academic performance with leadership. His story demonstrates that university success is not only about grades but also about taking responsibility, contributing to others, and developing leadership skills.

03
🇳🇬

Cambridge-Africa Nigerian PhD Student/Alumna

“Young People's Career Decisions and Self-Actualisation”

The researcher explored how young people make career decisions and how parents, teachers, education, and society influence those choices. Her work also examined challenges young researchers can experience, including uncertainty and self-doubt, while conducting research in Nigeria.

04
🇰🇪

Lynnet Hamblin

“From Kenya to UBC: Education, Growth and Community”

Lynnet described moving from Kenya to the University of British Columbia through the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program. Her experience showed how access to education can develop confidence, adaptability, leadership, and a desire to contribute to one's community.

05
🇿🇦

Matome Lethwane

“From Survival to Solution”

Matome explained how challenges faced by students inspired him to create a solution. His experience demonstrates how education and personal difficulties can motivate young people to develop practical ideas that help others.

06
🇨🇦

Sarah Mohamed

“Student Story: Sarah Mohamed”

Sarah explained how her academic and professional experiences helped her recognize the importance of understanding how education is designed and delivered. This realization influenced her decision to pursue further study in education and learning sciences.

07
🇨🇦

Hy Goldberg

“Made by Perseverance, Family and Education”

Hy's story emphasizes perseverance, family support, and education. His journey shows how education can provide a foundation for professional development while personal determination and family values continue to shape a person's success.

08
🇺🇸

Ben Bowman

“Coming Full-Circle: Student to Teacher”

Ben described how education transformed his own life and career. After experiencing the value of education himself, he returned to the field as a teacher, showing how someone's educational journey can inspire them to help the next generation.

09
🇺🇸

Lori Lander

“Education Changed My Life”

Lori shared her experience as a first-generation student and explained how education helped her overcome obstacles and create new possibilities. She also highlighted the importance of mentors and encouraging others who are pursuing higher education.

10
🇺🇸

Kristy Chau

“My Education Changed My Life”

Kristy described how education opened opportunities for her, including an internship that contributed to her career path. She emphasized staying focused, asking for help, managing time effectively, and remaining consistent.

11
🇬🇧

Olivia Butterfield

“My Time at Oxford Has Been Completely Transformative”

Olivia described how studying History at Oxford changed her academically and personally. Coming from a state-school background, she explained how university challenged her thinking and gave her new opportunities for intellectual and personal growth.

12
🇬🇧

Daphne Harries

“Student Story: Daphne Harries”

Daphne shared her journey from a Foundation Year into studying English at Oxford. As the first person in her family to attend university, she discussed how education opened opportunities for her, including creative writing and student publishing.

13
🇬🇧

Chloe Pomfret

“There's Inspiration All Around You”

Chloe discussed her difficult educational circumstances and how outreach opportunities helped her access higher education. Her story demonstrates how education can create possibilities even when someone's early circumstances are challenging.

14
🇬🇧

Alexandros Pamnani

“A Passion for Learning”

Alexandros described a non-linear educational journey that included leaving formal education, working as a mechanic, and later studying at Cambridge. His experience shows that educational journeys do not always follow one straight path and that curiosity can lead people back to learning.

15
🇩🇪

Ehsanullah Attiq

“Adapting to a New Educational Landscape: My Experience as an International Student”

Ehsanullah described adjusting to a different education system after interrupted education. He discussed language, new academic expectations, and developing new study habits while adapting to life as an international student in Germany.

16
🇩🇪

Akua Bobson

“Why Germany? Why Heidelberg?”

Akua explained why she chose Germany for further education after spending much of her life in Accra and studying at the University of Ghana. She was interested in experiencing a different educational system while also developing her German language and cultural understanding.

17
🇳🇱

Alena Kerosinskaya

“Don't Be Afraid to Fail, Be Afraid Not to Try”

Alena reflected on her career journey after completing her master's degree. Her story emphasizes experimentation, learning from different experiences, and not being afraid to try something new when building a career.

18
🇦🇺

Hasnain

“Hasnain's New Life”

Hasnain described beginning a new life in Australia after experiencing major challenges before arriving there. Education became an important part of his new path and helped him pursue his ambitions while building a future in a new country.

19
🇫🇷

Camille Gardent

“GBSB Student Story: Camille Gardent”

Camille shared her experience studying Communication and Future Marketing. Her story shows how higher education can help students develop professional knowledge and prepare themselves for careers in a changing global environment.

Shen Xiaodong

“Transforming Lives in Remote Mountains”

Shen Xiaodong described his work in education in a remote mountainous area. His story focuses on using education and leadership to serve students and communities that have fewer opportunities, demonstrating how learning can become a form of social contribution.

WHY STUDENT VOICES?

Learning becomes powerful when we hear the people living it.

Student Voices brings together experiences from different backgrounds and parts of the world. Each story offers a different lesson about perseverance, leadership, discovery, courage, education, community and the determination to build a better future.

Nketia-Marfo
A SPACE FOR STORIES

Every journey can teach us something.

I created Student Voices because students are more than grades, applications, certificates and academic titles.

Behind every educational journey is a person with questions, struggles, dreams, discoveries and experiences worth hearing.

These stories are here to remind us that learning can happen through people, places, challenges and unexpected opportunities.

— Nketia-Marfo
